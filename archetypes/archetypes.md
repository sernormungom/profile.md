# Job archetypes — CV tracks A–F

Use these tracks to classify a role by its actual responsibilities, then select relevant evidence from `profile.md`. These are employer requirements, not claims about Norberto's experience. The individual ad decides which requirements are mandatory; examples below are not universal gates.

| Track | Role | Main emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| A | Automotive Function Developer / Model-Based Control | Own a vehicle function through modelling, implementation and validation |
| B | Embedded C++ Platform & Application Engineer | Deliver embedded C++ and steer architecture and quality |
| C | Cloud Backend, DevOps & Developer Platform | Build, deploy and operate services and developer tooling |
| D | Applied AI / Agentic Systems Engineer | Build useful LLM applications, agents and integrations |
| E | MedTech / Regulated Device Software | Deliver device software under a quality system |
| F | Engineering Manager, Embedded & Control Systems | Develop people, allocate resources and secure engineering delivery |

## How to use the tracks

- Check conditions first: location, onsite days, employment form, travel, language, licences and clearance. Gothenburg-region roles are the focus; confirm conditions from each ad.
- Separate mandatory gates from daily responsibilities and optional differentiators. Show supported gates early in the CV.
- Grade evidence as **Named** (listed), **Applied** (used in a specific assignment), **Owned** (accountable for an outcome), or **Taught** (introduced a practice or trained others). Senior responsibilities generally need Owned or Taught evidence.
- Show behaviour through decisions, collaboration and delivery. Use concrete assignments and make relevant years of experience easy to see.
- Classify by the ad body: an embedded title may describe AI automation; an architect title may describe IT governance. Advisory work needs evidence of assessments and recommendations.
- Match the ad's language. Include AI-assisted engineering in A, B, C or F when relevant and supported; preserve the distinction between tooling, prototypes and production delivery.
- Missing mandatory evidence cannot be fixed by wording. Respect all profile limitations and distinguish transferable experience from direct domain experience.

## A — Automotive Function Developer / Model-Based Control

**Role:** Own a specialised vehicle function through requirements, model-based development, verification and calibration.

**Typical gates:** Relevant automotive/embedded seniority (often 7–12+ years), a relevant engineering degree, applied MATLAB/Simulink, automotive experience and depth in one vehicle domain. A strict automotive-years gate differs from one accepting broader embedded experience.

**Core work:**

- Full V-cycle: requirements, design, documentation, implementation and verification.
- Simulink models and production code generation with TargetLink or Embedded Coder.
- ECU functions and calibratable parameters; rig/vehicle tuning where required.
- MIL/SIL/HIL, unit and requirements-based tests.
- AUTOSAR Classic integration, Vector DaVinci, CAN tooling and diagnostics/UDS as required.
- Integrate other engineers' deliveries and coordinate suppliers and stakeholders.

**Differentiators and context:** Development/test/calibration automation; AI-assisted engineering; physics-informed ML; powertrain, thermal, steering, body or combustion specialisation. Relevant ecosystems include ISO 26262, ISO/SAE 21434, MISRA, V-model, SAFe, SystemWeaver, INCA and dSPACE. Check driving/test-track licences and travel separately.

**CV emphasis:** One domain in depth, a function delivered, test context and cross-team influence. Avoid implying vehicle calibration, code-generation ownership or safety-standard expertise from adjacent experience alone.

## B — Embedded C++ Platform & Application Engineer

**Role:** Deliver hand-written embedded C++ close to the platform, with architecture and quality responsibility.

**Typical gates:** Production C++ (often 5+ years), embedded Linux, automotive experience and sometimes a relevant degree or prior technical-lead role. Some roles require QNX or Android Automotive specifically.

**Core work:**

- Large embedded C++ systems, reviews and coding standards.
- Architecture, interfaces and requirements decomposition.
- Linux/POSIX, QNX, hypervisors or Android; AUTOSAR Classic/Adaptive where required.
- Test strategy: GoogleTest, desktop execution, HIL and vehicle testing.
- Target debugging, rigs and prototypes; CAN, Ethernet, SOME/IP and UDS.
- Embedded CI/CD with tools such as Jenkins, CMake, Git/Gerrit, Bazel, Zuul and Docker; Python tooling.

**Differentiators and context:** ADAS, sensor fusion, Kalman filters, trajectory planning, Euro NCAP, software-defined vehicles, OTA and configuration management. ISO 26262, ISO/SAE 21434, ASPICE, MISRA C++, static analysis and risk work such as FMEA/TARA/HARA may apply. Agentic coding and open-source work can distinguish candidates.

**CV emphasis:** Shipped code, target environment, architecture decisions, testing and technical leadership without formal authority. Avoid treating desktop C++, a tool name or a lead title as proof of embedded integration or architecture ownership.

## C — Cloud Backend, DevOps & Developer Platform

**Role:** Own services or developer platforms across development, deployment, operation and improvement. Tailor the stack to the ad.

**Typical gates:** Strong production depth in a primary language and cloud, often 5–7+ professional years. Many roles require production operations ownership. Stacks vary: Java/AWS, C++/Azure/OpenShift, or Python/TypeScript/Azure.

**Core work:**

- Backend APIs, REST/OpenAPI and microservices.
- Containers and orchestration: Docker, Kubernetes or OpenShift.
- CI/CD and automation; Git-based collaboration.
- Infrastructure as Code with CloudFormation, CDK or Terraform.
- Monitoring, logging, alerting, incident investigation and root-cause analysis.
- Full lifecycle, secure design and developer enablement; LLM/agent/MCP tooling where central to the role.

**Differentiators and context:** Internal developer platforms such as Backstage; serverless, queues, NoSQL, TDD, cloud migration and connected-vehicle services. Data-engineering variants may require Databricks/Spark/lakehouse depth and should be assessed separately.

**CV emphasis:** Named services, pipelines actually built, operational decisions and evidence colleagues adopted the platform. Do not equate basic cloud deployment with infrastructure or on-call ownership.

## D — Applied AI / Agentic Systems Engineer

**Role:** Build agents, LLM applications and automation that work with existing systems, and enable others to use them.

**Typical gates:** Strong Python, demonstrable hands-on LLM/agentic work and professional software experience; some roles specify a CS/Data Science/Software Engineering degree. Distinguish ads requiring production delivery from those accepting prototypes.

**Core work:**

- Agents calling tools/APIs, backend LLM services and engineering automation pipelines.
- Define agent capabilities and integrations with systems and data sources.
- Extract, clean and structure data with Python/SQL or domain-specific tools.
- Translate business needs into prototypes, evaluation, priorities and deployment plans.
- APIs such as FastAPI, containers, cloud and CI around AI applications.
- Enable teams through practices, configuration, training and guardrails.

**Differentiators and context:** MCP, Copilot Studio, Google ADK, MLOps, NumPy/Pandas or BigQuery where relevant. Choose the application domain: engineering workflows, finance automation or regulated clinical software. Regulated roles may require IEC 62304, ISO 13485, EU MDR, EU AI Act or model governance.

**CV emphasis:** Concrete artefacts, personal contribution, use by others and evaluation. Internal tooling can be relevant evidence. Preserve prototype status and implementation/measurement limits; courses, strategic advice and AI-generated code do not alone establish production AI or model-training expertise.

## E — MedTech / Regulated Device Software

**Role:** Build C++ device software and interfaces under a quality system, with traceability and communication with end users.

**Typical gates:** Strong C++, applied Qt/QML or QWidgets, and prior MedTech or another explicitly accepted regulated domain. Strict MedTech requirements are not satisfied merely by other safety-critical work. Some roles require a local base and engineering degree.

**Core work:**

- Embedded C++/Linux applications and device user interfaces.
- Formal documentation, requirements traceability, verification and risk analysis.
- Work within a quality management system and defined development lifecycle.
- Architecture decisions, complex stakeholder coordination and field issue escalation.
- Communicate with clinicians and other non-technical users.

**Differentiators and context:** IEC 62304, ISO 13485, EU MDR and V-model; simulation/control/HIL; offshore partners and clinical-site travel. AI-enabled device roles may add Python/ML, APIs, containers, model governance and safe coding-tool adoption.

**CV emphasis:** Actual interface delivery, named standards personally worked with, documentation discipline and user context. Avoid presenting general regulated experience as medical-device delivery or Qt exposure as a shipped interface.

## F — Engineering Manager, Embedded & Control Systems

**Role:** Hire and develop engineers, set priorities, allocate resources and secure delivery while retaining enough technical depth to guide design, quality and risk.

**Typical gates:** Proven people leadership, explicit line management where required, depth in the team's technical domain and sometimes an engineering degree or specified domain seniority. Management recency, language, travel and clearance are ad-specific checks.

**Core work:**

- Recruitment, coaching, competence growth and performance/appraisal processes.
- Priorities, staffing, resource planning, milestones and delivery follow-up.
- Cross-functional representation and stakeholder alignment.
- Technical direction through requirements, design reviews and test results.
- Engineering-process improvement, quality and risk handling.
- Budget/cost follow-up, suppliers, consultants, roadmaps and portfolio work where required.

**Differentiators and context:** AI-enabled engineering, industrial AI, Lean/Six Sigma, international teams, proposals/tenders and specialist responsibility alongside management. Embedded/control environments may use ISO 26262, V-model, agile/SAFe, configuration management and ISO 9001.

**CV emphasis:** Team size, decisions, hiring/development, resourcing and delivery. Show continued leadership after older management roles. Distinguish project leadership from line management, salary allocation from full budget/P&L ownership, and technical oversight from personal implementation. Swedish work-environment responsibility must be evidenced separately.
