# Archetype F — Engineering Manager, Embedded & Control Systems

Oct 29, 2026 · same six-layer model as Archetypes A–E in "../Job Requirement Archetypes — 5 CV Tracks.md", kept in its own file. Included in job-search screening alongside A–E.

**Built from 8 ads** (Platsbanken, Gothenburg region, fetched 2026-10-29): Aurobay Engineering Manager Diagnostic CORE and System Safety (31557139), Aurobay Engineering Manager for Calibration Leaders in Control & Diagnostics (31554718), Benifex Engineering Manager (31568768), Valmet Manager EI&C Engineering (31632064), RISE Gruppchef inom automation med specialistansvar (31655766), Frost Unmanned Head of Engineering (31544027), Cebon Industrial Head of Engineering (31529414), SHT Automation Process Engineering Manager (31183156). Adjacent, not counted: Autocom Senior Technical Project Manager (31552193), a delivery role without line management.

The two Aurobay ads are the inspiration and the closest fit; the other six keep the archetype from becoming a copy of one ad. Domain varies by ad (powertrain control, automation, EI&C, batteries, UAVs, web platform), but the *shape* of the role is the same: lead people, secure delivery, keep enough technical depth to steer design and risk.

**One-line pitch the CV must make:** *I lead engineers who build control and embedded software: I hire and grow them, set priorities, secure delivery, and keep the technical depth to steer design, quality and risk.*

## Layer 0 · Conditions

Gothenburg region, onsite or hybrid, permanent employment (none of the eight is a consulting assignment). English is the working language in 6/8; Valmet requires Swedish and RISE writes its ad in Swedish. Travel: Valmet at least 20%, Cebon monthly within Europe. Frost requires Swedish security clearance (säkerhetsprövning).

## Layer 1 · Gates

| Gate | Weight | Proof needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proven people leadership; explicit line management in 3/8 (Aurobay ×2, RISE) | 8/8 | Owned — title, team size, what you decided (hiring, appraisals, pay) |
| Technical background in the team's domain | 8/8 | Owned — one domain shown in depth, not a list |
| Engineering degree in a relevant field | 4/8 | Named degree and field |
| Stated years in the domain (5+ Valmet, 10+ SHT) | 2/8 | Years visible without arithmetic |

## Layer 2 · Core

| Requirement | Weight | Proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead and develop people: recruit, coach, competence growth, appraisals | 8/8 | Owned |
| Delivery: priorities, resource planning, milestones, follow-up | 8/8 | Owned |
| Cross-functional representation: section leadership team, forums, stakeholders | 8/8 | Owned |
| Technical direction: requirements, design reviews, test results | 6/8 | Owned |
| Ways of working and continuous improvement of the engineering process | 6/8 | Taught |
| Quality and risk handling | 5/8 | Owned |
| Budget, cost estimation and business judgement | 4/8 | Applied |
| Suppliers, external consultants and partners | 3/8 | Applied |
| Strategy, technical roadmap, project portfolio | 3/8 | Applied |

## Layer 3 · Differentiators

AI-enabled ways of working (Benifex names it as a role goal; RISE wants industrial AI). Lean / Six Sigma (SHT). Functional safety or regulatory compliance (Aurobay Diagnostic CORE: ISO 26262; Cebon: regulatory compliance). International, multi-site collaboration (Valmet, Benifex, SHT). Proposals, tenders and customer-facing technical work (RISE, Cebon, Frost). Specialist depth kept alongside the manager role (RISE "med specialistansvar", Cebon, Frost "stay hands-on where it is needed").

## Layer 4 · Domain & process

Varies by ad; for the embedded/control line: ISO 26262, V-model, agile/SAFe, configuration and version control, quality systems (ISO 9001), Lean / Six Sigma. Vocabulary these ads use: line management, resource planning, milestone reviews, budget follow-up, competence supply (kompetensförsörjning), work environment (arbetsmiljö), leadership team.

## Layer 5 · Behaviour

Creating clarity is the common thread (Aurobay "create clarity across ... interfaces", RISE "skapar tydlighet"). Collaborative and inclusive leadership (Aurobay, RISE, Valmet). Ownership and accountability (Valmet, Benifex, SHT). Removing obstacles for the team (Aurobay, Benifex). Hands-on where needed, but the main job is to lead (Frost, Cebon, SHT). Balancing technical priorities against cost, time and the organisation (Aurobay Calibration Leaders).

## Anti-signals

A manager title with no team size or no decisions named. Leadership shown only through adjectives. An individual-contributor CV with "leadership" in the skills list. Project management offered as people management for a line role. Technical depth with no delivery or business outcome attached. Management experience that is old with nothing since that shows leadership (recency is probed).
