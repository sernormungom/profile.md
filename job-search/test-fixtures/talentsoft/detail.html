The Position 

As Digital Product Compliance Specialist, you will play a key role in ensuring that our products comply with evolving digital regulations and standards. Working under the responsibility of the Digital Policy Manager, you will monitor regulatory developments and assess their impact on. Toyota Material Handling Europes products, with a particular focus on areas such as artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, connectivity and digital services. 

Working closely with R&D, IT/IS and other functions across the organisation, you will provide expert guidance on digital product requirements and drive the development of tools, processes and procedures that support compliance across our product portfolio. 

You will also represent TMHE in discussions with external stakeholders and contribute to the development of digital standards and regulations, helping shape the regulatory landscape in which our products operate. 

  

Your Responsibilities 

  • Monitor new and evolving digital regulations and standards, assessing their impact on TMHE’s products and identifying relevant compliance requirements. 
  • Provide expert guidance and support to the organisation on digital product requirements, particularly within artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, connectivity and digital services. 
  • Develop and maintain tools, processes and procedures to support product compliance with applicable digital regulations and standards. 
  • As part of R&D, IT/IS and other relevant functions to ensure a coordinated approach to digital product compliance. 
  • Represent TMHE in the development of digital standards and regulations, engaging with external organisations, industry stakeholders and consultants. 
  • Support relevant MSCOs on digital product compliance matters when needed and maintain strong relationships across TMHE and the wider Toyota organisation. 

Your Profile

Your Profile 

  • You have a university degree in Cybersecurity, Information Management or a related field. 
  • You have a couple of years of experience in a relevant field, with knowledge of digital regulations and standards. 
  • You have a good understanding of product software, telematics, robotics and systems engineering. 
  • You take an analytical, structured and meticulous approach to your work, with a strong team-oriented mindset. 
  • You have excellent relationship-building skills and enjoy collaborating with stakeholders across different departments. 
  • You are confident in preparing and delivering training sessions to stakeholders across Toyota Material Handling Group (TMHG). 
  • Fluent in English
